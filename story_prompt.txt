# PROMPT: THE DOUBLE-LIFE OF A REDO-DEVELOPER

**Genre:** Contemporary Fantasy / Time-Slip / Psychological Drama / Comedy
**Tonal Inspiration:** *The Greatest Estate Developer* (Comedy/Face-play), *I’m an Infinite Regressor, But I’ve Got Stories to Tell* (Emotional weight/Quiet interference), *Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint* (The "Void" / Bittersweet ending).

---

### **1. THE PREMISE**
Alex is a 30-year-old certified failure. Dead-end job, regrets like pancakes, and a life on autopilot. One night, blackout drunk and slurring at the ceiling, he makes a "hard wish": *"Yo universe... just yeet me back to 15-year-old me for one day so I can actually do something right... I'll even let you keep my adult body as hostage."*

**The Mechanic:**
- Every time Alex sleeps in one body, his consciousness swaps to the other.
- 15yo Alex (Past) <---> 30yo Alex (Present).
- It’s not a dream. Actions in the past ripple forward instantly. If he blows up a beaker in chemistry class at 15, he wakes up at 30 to find his high school group chats blowing up with memes about "that legend who blew up the lab."
- The timelines run parallel; he is living two lives simultaneously, ping-ponging every sleep cycle.

---

### **2. CHARACTERS & DYNAMICS**

#### **Alex (The Protagonist)**
- **Vibe:** Pure *Greatest Estate Developer* energy. His inner monologue is snarky, chaotic, and obsessed with "optimizing" his high school life using adult knowledge. He makes absurd "scammer" faces when he’s planning a "fix."
- **Goal:** Initially, just "not be a loser." Later, it becomes about "quietly interfering" in the lives of people he knows will fail (like his burnout friend **Jake**, or his struggling teacher) to save them without taking credit.

#### **Mia (The "Unattainable" Love Interest)**
- **Vibe:** The quiet girl with the sketchbook. In the present, she’s a warm, successful indie illustrator who creates comics about "the boy who changed after chemistry class."
- **The Twist (The Wall):** Alex’s primary motivation for "fixing" his life is often Mia. However, he makes the **painful discovery** that Mia is deeply, quietly in love with **"The Stranger"**—a guy Alex never knew existed in his original timeline. This guy isn't a bully or a loser; he's someone genuinely good, making it impossible for Alex to "scam" his way into her heart without destroying her happiness. This creates a love interest he *cannot* chase, adding to the "ORV Void."

#### **Kai (The "L-Style" Genius Girl)**
- **Visuals:** Pale, messy black hair, oversized hoodie, always has a lollipop in her mouth. Sits in the back of chemistry class like a detective.
- **Personality:** Hyper-observant, genius-level pattern recognition. She is the "L" (Death Note) of this story.
- **Role:** She is the only one who suspects Alex. She notices his "future knowledge," his "eyes changing," and his "statistically impossible" ripples.
- **The Secret:** Kai is a **love interest for Alex**, but it is **NEVER explicitly stated**. Her affection is shown through her quiet alliance, her "detective" obsession with him, and the way she carries the weight of his secret with him.
- **Backstory (The Tragedy):** Kai’s parents are a source of deep psychological trauma. Her father was a cold, celebrated mathematician who treated every conversation like a proof to be solved, while her artist mother left because she couldn’t breathe in that "data-only" household. Kai is left with a single sketchbook page from her mother: "Draw what you actually feel, not what they expect." Her father treats her genius like a tool, not a daughter. This creates an "Infinite Regressor" style tragedy—the heavy, hollow feeling of a child who was never actually *seen*.

---

### **3. THE EMOTIONAL CORE & TONE**

- **The Comedy:** Use absurd, over-the-top reactions and internal monologues. Alex should approach social situations like he’s "developing" an estate—calculating the "ROI" of a conversation or "scamming" his way into a better future for his friends.
- **The Quiet Interference:** Like *I'm an Infinite Regressor*, Alex starts keeping a secret "Swap Journal" where he documents the lives he is quietly altering. He sees the "failed versions" of his classmates—the bully heading for prison, the teacher facing a divorce—and makes tiny, invisible nudges to save them. He takes no credit. He carries the heavy, emotional burden of knowing their original tragedies, becoming a "guardian angel" whose work is entirely unrecorded.
- **The Relationship with Kai:** Kai becomes his "co-pilot." She is the only one who sees the toll it takes on him. Their bond is built on the shared burden of "seeing too much."

---

### **4. THE "ORV" ENDING (THE VOID)**
The story should lead to a **bittersweet conclusion**.
- Alex succeeds. He "fixes" his life, saves his friends, and creates a "perfect" 30-year-old present.
- **The ORV Void hits:** Alex realizes that by rewriting his destiny, the "original" version of himself—the pathetic loser who made the drunk wish—has been erased. He is now the "Only Reader" of his own life’s original, failed draft.
- **The Final Scene:** He stands in a "perfect" present. Everyone he loves is thriving because of him, but he feels a massive, hollow gap in his chest. Kai is the only one who can see this hole. She is there, the only other person who remembers the "lost" timeline, standing beside him in the silence of a story that only they have read.

---

### **WRITING INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AI:**
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Especially regarding Kai's feelings for Alex. It should be in the way she watches him, the notes she leaves him, and her protective nature.
- **Balance the Tones:** Jump from a hilarious, face-distorting comedy beat to a heavy, heart-wrenching realization about a friend’s future.
- **The Chemistry Class:** Always use the chemistry class as the "anchor" point for the past.
- **The "Another Guy":** Make Mia’s love for this stranger feel real and insurmountable, a "wall" that Alex’s time-travel can’t break.

---
**GOAL:** Write a story that makes the reader laugh out loud one moment and stare at a wall in existential silence the next.
