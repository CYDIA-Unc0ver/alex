# PROMPT: THE REDO-DEVELOPER'S DOUBLE LIFE

**Genre:** Contemporary Fantasy / Time-Slip / Psychological Drama / Dark Comedy
**Tone:** A volatile mix of theatrical, exaggerated comedy and heavy, soul-crushing existentialism. The story should feel like a high-stakes scam that slowly reveals itself to be a tragedy.

---

### **1. THE PREMISE & HARD RULES**
Alex is a 30-year-old failure who makes a slurred, drunk wish for a "redo." He wakes up in his 15-year-old body in chemistry class, but his 30-year-old self still exists.

**The Mechanics of the "Double Life":**
- **The Swap:** Consciousness is tied to sleep. When Alex falls asleep in one body, he instantly wakes up in the other.
- **The Stasis:** The body not currently inhabited by Alex’s consciousness enters a deep, unshakeable "stasis" (a coma-like sleep). This creates logistical nightmares in both timelines (e.g., 30yo Alex "oversleeping" for 14 hours while 15yo Alex is at school).
- **Time Flow:** Time moves at a strict 1:1 ratio. If he spends a full day in the past, a full day has passed in the present.
- **Memory Sync:** Alex retains perfect, vivid memory of both lives. The mental strain of "living twice" causes sensory bleed-through (e.g., smelling chemistry chemicals while sitting in his 30yo apartment).
- **The Death Stake:** If either body dies, the connection snaps. Alex’s consciousness is permanently trapped in the remaining body, and the other timeline is erased or becomes a "dead" world.

---

### **2. CHARACTER BEHAVIORS & DYNAMICS**

#### **Alex (The "Developer")**
- **The "Business" Mindset:** Alex treats social interactions like high-stakes business deals. He internally assigns "Potential ROI" to classmates and "Risk Factors" to his boss or teachers.
- **Theatrical Defensiveness:** To hide his genuine care, Alex adopts an exaggerated, borderline villainous persona when "fixing" someone's life. He uses distorted "scammer" grins, dramatic flourishes, and snarky narration.
- **Invisible Guardian:** He operates through manipulation and "invisible nudges." He saves people by orchestrating "accidents" that lead to positive outcomes, never allowing himself to take the credit.

#### **Kai (The Genius Investigator)**
- **Visuals:** A girl with pale skin, messy black hair, oversized hoodies, and a permanent lollipop. She sits in the back of class, watching the room like a crime scene.
- **Active Agency:** Kai is not a passive observer; she is Alex’s primary obstacle and eventual co-pilot. She **actively tests** him—planting false news to see if he "corrects" her, or tracking the exact minute he falls asleep to prove the 1:1 time swap.
- **Philosophical Conflict:** She frequently challenges Alex’s "interventions." She argues that by "fixing" people, he is stealing their right to grow through failure. This creates a constant, sharp tension between them.
- **The Silent Love:** Her affection for Alex is rooted in the "broken adult" she sees behind his teenage eyes. It is shown through her intellectual rivalry and the way she quietly covers for his "stasis" periods in the past.

#### **Mia (The Unreachable Anchor)**
- **The Stranger (Ethan):** Mia is in love with **Ethan**, a quiet, observant boy who wasn't part of Alex's original memories. Ethan isn't a rival to be defeated; he is genuinely the "better half" for Mia.
- **The Breaking Point:** Alex witnesses a moment where Ethan notices a tiny, hidden detail in Mia’s sketchbook—a detail that Alex, despite all his "future knowledge" and planning, completely overlooked. This moment breaks Alex’s confidence, proving that some things cannot be "developed" or forced.

---

### **3. THE EMOTIONAL CORE: THE "ONLY READER"**

- **The Swap Journal:** Alex keeps a secret journal documenting the "original draft" of the people around him—the burnout friend **Jake** who was a criminal, the teacher who was a drunk. He is the only person in the world who remembers their suffering.
- **Kai’s Tragedy:** Her father is a cold mathematician who treats her like a calculation; her mother is an artist who fled the "cold" home. Kai is haunted by her mother’s last note: *"Draw what you actually feel, not what they expect."* This is why she is obsessed with Alex—he is the only "real" thing in her calculated world.

---

### **4. THE ENDING: THE SHARPER KNIFE**
- **The "Perfect" Prison:** Alex succeeds. His 30yo life is wealthy and successful. Jake is a hero. Mia is happy with Ethan.
- **The Tangible Loss:** To achieve this, Alex had to sacrifice a specific, cherished memory. In the finale, he tries to tell a "loser" joke that used to make Jake howl with laughter in the original timeline. In this new world, Jake just smiles politely, not understanding the reference.
- **Final Image:** Alex and Kai standing in the silence of a "perfect" world. They are the only two people who know that this reality is a second draft. Kai hands him a lollipop, and they stand together as the only two "readers" of a story that has been erased from everyone else’s minds.

---

### **AI WRITING INSTRUCTIONS:**
1. **Show, Don't Tell:** Never state Kai likes Alex. Show it through her guarding his sleeping body or her sharp, testing questions.
2. **Balance Tones:** A scene should start with a hilarious "scammer" face and end with a quiet, devastating realization about the weight of time.
3. **The Journal:** Use brief, haunting excerpts from Alex's journal to bridge the two timelines.
4. **Ethan's Presence:** Make Ethan feel like a natural, immovable part of Mia's world, making Alex’s unrequited love feel like an inevitable law of physics.
